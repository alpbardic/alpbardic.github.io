Selected Projects

  • CNN compression for an ESP32
    ECE 594 team project · ESP32-WROOM with 312 KB of DRAM
    Fit a CNN onto the chip with structured pruning, adaptive pooling, and 8-bit quantization, so inference runs on the device itself. Chose the model and the compression methods, trained it, and exported the weights the team deployed.
    Oct 2025 – Dec 2025
  • Reinforcement learning MAC protocol in ns-3
    ECE 533 Advanced Network Communications · University of Illinois Chicago
    Built a Q-learning agent that picks each node's transmission slot from its own queue length and timing feedback. Connected it to an ns-3 simulation with ns3-gym, where it beat both the random-slot and fixed-slot baselines.
    Oct 2025 – Dec 2025
  • Reducing the power consumption of refrigerators
    Senior industrial design project · Bilkent University & Arçelik
    Built a reinforcement learning model with the software team to lower power use. Worked with the sensor design team on the ice sensor.
    Sep 2021 – May 2022
  • Image captioning with CNNs and RNNs
    EEE443 Neural Networks · Bilkent University
    Combined CNNs and RNNs to generate captions for test images, then tuned the networks and measured how accurate the captions were.
    Oct 2021 – Dec 2021
