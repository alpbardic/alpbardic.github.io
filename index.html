About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Prof. Erdem Koyuncu. I work on distributed and decentralized machine learning.

How do you train and run a model on many small devices, when each one has limited compute, memory, and bandwidth, and no device is in charge?

Random Walking Snakes, my decentralized training method, converges faster than Random Walk Learning. Decentralized model-distributed inference splits one model across many devices and uses graph coloring to keep their transfers from colliding, which lowers communication latency.

Right now I am working on gossip algorithms for machine learning, wildfire hotspot detection, and distributed world models. I also teach as a graduate assistant for machine learning, deep learning, and pattern recognition.

If you work on similar problems, write to me. I am glad to talk.
